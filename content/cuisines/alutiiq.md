The Alutiiq, or Sugpiaq, live from Prince William Sound to Kodiak Island and the Alaska Peninsula. Like their Unangax̂ neighbors, their food is tied to the water: [[Salmon]] smoked and dried, halibut, seal, shellfish and seaweed, with berries and greens from the land.
