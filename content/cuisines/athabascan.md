Athabascan peoples live across Interior Alaska, and their lives follow the big rivers like the Yukon and Tanana, which serve as roads and as a source of food. [[Salmon]] and [[Whitefish]] are caught and dried in summer, [[Moose]] and [[Caribou]] are hunted in fall, and berries are picked and put away.

The University of Alaska Museum's guide calls sharing the most important part of Athabascan subsistence living. People who have moved away still return home to harvest.
