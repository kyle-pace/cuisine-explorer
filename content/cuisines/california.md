[[Acorn|Acorns]] are a staple for many California nations. They are cracked, pounded into flour with a stone mortar and pestle, and washed with water to leach out the bitter tannins. The flour is cooked into [[Acorn mush]] in a watertight basket with hot stones, stirred with a wooden paddle so it doesn't burn. Hupa, Pomo, Karuk and Yurok weavers still make acorn baskets, and carvers still make mush paddles.

Cooks today bring the same foods to new tables. In Berkeley, Vincent Medina (Muwekma Ohlone) and Louis Trevino (Rumsen Ohlone) run Café Ohlone, serving acorn soup and acorn flour brownies alongside venison, chia, mushrooms and native berries.
