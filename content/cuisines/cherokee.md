Cherokee people live in northeastern Oklahoma, home of the Cherokee Nation, and in western North Carolina, home of the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians. [[Kanuchi]], a soup made from pounded hickory nuts, is still served at traditional tables and stomp grounds in Oklahoma, and the nuts are gathered in fall before the first frost.

Spring wild onion dinners bring wild onions and eggs together with [[Hominy|hominy]], beans, grape dumplings, kanuchi, fry bread and corn bread. In North Carolina, cooks simmer a [[Three Sisters]] stew of corn, beans and squash, and make an old style chestnut bread: corn cooked with wood ash, ground, mixed with chestnuts, wrapped in leaves and boiled.
