[[Salmon]], berries, elk and roots are the core of the Coast Salish diet around the Salish Sea. Muckleshoot guidelines call for a traditional meal like roasted salmon once a week, and intertribal [[Camas]] harvests bring Elders and youth together.

The Muckleshoot Food Sovereignty Project brings huckleberries, nettles, camas, salmon and elk back into everyday meals through classes, gardens and seminars, and cooks try new forms too, like elk burgers and huckleberry smoothies. At Nisqually, a community garden gives citizens free produce and syrups made from nettle, elderberry and fir tips.
