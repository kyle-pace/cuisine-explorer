For the Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee, in the James Bay region of Quebec, the year follows the hunt. In spring, goose break empties the towns as families head to their bush camps, children learn to call geese, and goose is roasted slowly over an open fire for hours, twisting on a cord as it cooks.

[[Moose]], [[Caribou]], bear, beaver, snowshoe hare, ducks, ptarmigan and fish are all traditional foods, along with blueberries, cranberries, raspberries and strawberries. The Cree Nation Government and Cree Board of Health track how a changing climate affects each of them.
