The Garinagu were exiled from St. Vincent and settled along the Caribbean coast of Central America, and the largest Garifuna community outside Central America is in New York City, mostly in the South Bronx. Their kitchen runs on [[Cassava]], green and ripe [[Plantain (cooking)|plantains]], green bananas, [[Coconut milk]] and fish.

[[Casabe|Ereba]], cassava bread, is women's work that takes days: the roots are peeled, grated and strained, and the flour is spread in thin rounds on a hot griddle, then shared with family and neighbors. Its color is the yellow stripe on the Garifuna flag. [[Hudut]] is plantain pounded smooth and served with falmo, fish soup made with coconut milk. Darasa are [[Tamale|tamales]] made with green banana instead of corn, and durudia are thick tortillas made with coconut milk.
