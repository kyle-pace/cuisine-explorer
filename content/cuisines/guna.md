On the islands of Guna Yala, off Panama's Caribbean coast, meals center on the sea. Tule masi is a soup of fish and [[Plantain (cooking)|plantain]], fish caught the same day comes with coconut rice, and coconut water is drunk from a nut cut from the palm just before.

Fish is still a daily food for many island families, and many have [[Cacao|cacao]] every day, though researchers working on Ustupu and Ogobsucum found fried and packaged foods spreading fast. At big festivals, women stir huge pots of [[Chicha]] made from sugarcane juice, herbs and corn.
