The Kaigani Haida live on southern Prince of Wales Island, around Hydaburg, with most Haida across the border in Haida Gwaii. Like their Tlingit neighbors, they eat from the forest and the sea: [[Salmon]], halibut, shellfish, seaweed, [[Herring]] eggs and berries.
