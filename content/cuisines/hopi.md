On the mesas of northeastern Arizona, Hopi farmers grow corn, and [[Blue corn]] is the base of [[Piki]], a paper-thin bread of cornmeal, ash and water spread by hand on a hot stone. Hopi women make it, a skill that takes years to learn, and a well kept piki stone passes from mother to daughter.

Piki is everyday food, easy to carry to the fields, and visitors are often offered a fresh roll. It also marks weddings, baby namings and other ceremonies.
