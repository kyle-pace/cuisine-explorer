The Iñupiat live across Alaska's far north and northwest, from the Arctic coast to the Kobuk and Noatak rivers. Life there still turns around the [[Whale]], walrus, [[Seal oil|seal]], [[Caribou]] and fish.

Whaling communities share [[Muktuk]], whale skin with its layer of fat, through the year, and frozen or dried fish dipped in [[Seal oil]] is an everyday food. [[Akutaq]], whipped fat mixed with berries, is made for gatherings.
