Métis food grew out of the buffalo hunt and the fur trade on the Prairies. [[Bison]] was the main meat, dried into jerky or pounded and mixed with fat and berries as [[Pemmican]], the food of voyageurs and winter travelers. [[Rubaboo]] is a stew of peas or corn thickened with pemmican, and boulettes, meatballs of moose or elk, simmer in broth.

[[Bannock]], or galette, a flat bread of flour, water and fat, is baked on a griddle, on a stick or in a pan by the fire, and fried as beignes. Chokecherries, saskatoons, blueberries and cranberries are picked through summer, fish like [[Whitefish]] and goldeye are smoked, and [[Wild rice]] goes into pancakes.
