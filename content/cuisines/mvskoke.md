Corn runs through Mvskoke cooking. [[Sofkee|Osafke]], or sofkee, is a drink made from hominy corn and lye, the lye dripped through hardwood ash, ideally from black jack or oak. Cvtvhakv are blue dumplings of corn dough colored with the ash of burned purple pea hulls, and grape dumplings use wild possum grapes picked in fall.

Indian pumpkin, a squash Mvskoke ancestors have grown for thousands of years, is fried with sugar until it caramelizes. The Mvskoke Food Sovereignty Initiative grows old osafke corn and Indian pumpkin from saved seed, and elders teach young people to make osafke.
