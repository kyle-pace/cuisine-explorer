Along the west coast of Vancouver Island, [[Salmon]] is the most important fish, smoked on sticks to keep through the year. When [[Herring]] spawn, cedar branches are sunk near the spawning grounds and the eggs that coat them are peeled off and eaten. Roe on kelp is gathered too, and whole herring are smoked with head and tail on. Hesquiaht's name comes from eating herring roe off eelgrass.

Clams, shellfish and sea mammals come from the shore, deer from inland, and berries, shoots and [[Camas]] bulbs are gathered in spring and summer. The west coast of Vancouver Island has had no commercial herring fishery since 2005; it's open only to First Nations food, social and ceremonial harvests.
