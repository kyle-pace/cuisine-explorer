The people of Gambell and Savoonga, on St. Lawrence Island in the Bering Sea, share their language with Yupik communities across the strait in Chukotka. The Alaska Native Heritage Center groups them with the Iñupiaq as people of the north, and the sea feeds them: walrus, [[Whale]] and seal, with greens and berries from the island in summer.
