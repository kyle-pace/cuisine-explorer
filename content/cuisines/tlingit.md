The Tlingit live along Southeast Alaska's coast and islands, where food comes from both the forest and the sea. [[Salmon]] is the center of the year, smoked and dried for winter. In spring, [[Herring]] eggs are gathered on hemlock branches or kelp, and [[Eulachon]] are rendered into a rich oil.
