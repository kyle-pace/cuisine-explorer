The Tohono O'odham, the Desert People, live in the Sonoran Desert of southern Arizona. Their food comes from farming, gathering and hunting: [[Tepary bean|tepary beans]], squash, melons and chiles grown in fields watered by summer monsoon floods, and wild foods like cholla buds, prickly pear pads, mesquite beans and [[Acorn|acorns]].

In midsummer, before the rains, families gather [[Saguaro fruit]] with long poles made from saguaro ribs. The harvest marks the start of the O'odham new year, and the pulp becomes syrup, jam and wine. Tohono O'odham Community Action works to bring back traditional farming and foods, which are high in fiber and help keep blood sugar steady.
