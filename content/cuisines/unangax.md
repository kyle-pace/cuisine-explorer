The Unangax̂ have lived along the Aleutian chain and the Pribilof Islands for thousands of years, and their food comes from the water around them: [[Salmon]], halibut and cod, seal and sea lion, sea urchins, and plants and berries from the islands. Russian contact also left its mark, so tea and fish pies sit alongside older foods.
