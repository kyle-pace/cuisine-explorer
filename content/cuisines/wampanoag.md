Wampanoag gardens in southeastern New England grow [[Maize|corn]], beans and squash together, and the first green corn comes in midsummer. Corn is cooked into [[Nasaump]], a thick, filling dish sometimes made with nuts and berries. The shore gives [[Clam|clams]], quahogs, oysters, mussels and lobsters, rivers and the sea give herring, cod, bass and eels, and deer is hunted with every part put to use.

Giving thanks for food mattered to the Wampanoag and still does. Mashpee Wampanoag chef Sherry Pocknett, the first Indigenous woman to win a James Beard Award, cooks quahog chowder, three sisters [[Succotash]] and corn cakes with cranberries.
