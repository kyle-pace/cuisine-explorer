Yup'ik and Cup'ik people live along Alaska's southwest coast and up the Yukon and Kuskokwim rivers. Summer is fish camp, when [[Salmon]] is cut, dried and smoked to last the winter, and fall is for berries and greens from the tundra.

[[Akutaq]], from a Yup'ik word for "mixed together," is whipped fat with berries and sometimes fish. Dried fish dipped in [[Seal oil]] is a favorite at any time of year.
