Akutaq, a Yup'ik word for "mixed together," is fat whipped by hand until it's light and creamy, then folded with berries, and sometimes fish or meat. What goes in depends on the place and the season: [[Seal oil]] and sea mammal fat on the coast, [[Caribou]] or [[Moose]] fat inland. It's made for gatherings, potlatches and holidays.
