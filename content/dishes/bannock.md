A flat bread of flour, water and fat, often raised with baking powder, cooked on a griddle, on a stick over the fire, or in a pan. [[Métis foodways|Métis]] cooks call it galette, and it's served with fry bread at Anishinaabe sugarbush gatherings at Grand Portage.
