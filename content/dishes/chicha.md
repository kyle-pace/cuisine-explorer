A name for many fermented and fresh drinks made by Indigenous peoples across South and Central America. [[Amazonian Kichwa foodways|Kichwa]] women make it from boiled [[Cassava|yuca]], chewed so saliva starts the fermentation, and welcome guests with a bowl. [[Guna foodways|Guna]] women stir chicha of sugarcane juice, herbs and corn for festivals, and the [[Wayuu foodways|Wayuu]] make uujolu, a corn chicha without alcohol, for hot days and marriage proposals.
