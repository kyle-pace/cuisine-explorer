Small breads of [[Cassava|manioc]] flour with lard, eggs, milk, anise and often a hard, salty farmer's cheese, sold in Paraguay by vendors called chiperas and baked in a tatakua, a brick and mud oven with a [[Guaraní foodways|Guaraní]] name. Mbyá Guaraní women in Brazil make a chipa of corn, too.
