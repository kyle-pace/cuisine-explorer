Pork marinated in [[Achiote|achiote]] and sour orange, wrapped in banana leaves and slow cooked, traditionally underground in a pib. The [[Maya cuisine|Maya]] cooked birds and deer this way and took up pork soon after the Spanish brought pigs. It's served shredded with warm tortillas, pickled red onion and a little habanero.
