A feast cooked in a pit on Chiloé Island, Chile. Red-hot stones go in first, then meats, clams and mussels, native potatoes and the potato breads milcao and chapalele, all sealed under Chilean rhubarb leaves to steam for about 90 minutes. In Yaldad, a Huilliche community cooks it with shellfish its own members gather at low tide, and a cooking pit on the island has been dated to 6,000 years ago.
