Green and ripe [[Plantain (cooking)|plantains]], boiled, pounded smooth in a hana and shaped into a ball, served with fish soup in [[Coconut milk|coconut milk]]. Strictly, hudut is the plantain, and it's a dish that brings [[Garifuna foodways|Garifuna]] families together.
