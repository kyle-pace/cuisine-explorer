A spicy red turkey soup from the Q'eqchi' [[Maya cuisine|Maya]] of Alta Verapaz, Guatemala, made with tomatoes, chiles, cilantro and [[Achiote|achiote]]. It goes back before the Spanish conquest, it's eaten at weddings and other big days, and Guatemala declared it part of its intangible heritage in 2007.
