Thick sauces of [[Chili pepper|chiles]], seeds and spices. In Oaxaca, [[Zapotec foodways|Zapotec]] cook Abigail Mendoza Ruiz serves a pre-Hispanic mole with corn, tomato and yerba santa, [[Mixtec foodways|Mixtec]] cooks make mole de guajes, and each October the goat matanza in the Tehuacán valley yields mole de caderas.
