Dried meat, usually [[Bison]], pounded fine and mixed with fat and often dried berries. It keeps for months and travels well. The [[Lakota foodways|Lakota]] make it as wasna, often with chokecherries, and it fed [[Métis foodways|Métis]] voyageurs and winter travelers.
