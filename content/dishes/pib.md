A big filled corn dough dish, also called mucbipollo, wrapped in banana leaves and buried for hours in an earth oven of fire-heated stones. [[Maya cuisine|Maya]] families in Yucatán make it for Hanal Pixán, and the first pib out of the ground goes on the altar for relatives who have died.
