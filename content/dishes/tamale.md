Dough wrapped in a leaf and steamed. [[Maya cuisine|Maya]] cooks in Guatemala wrap corn dough in flat maxan leaves, [[Purépecha foodways|Purépecha]] cocineras make corundas and fresh corn uchepos, [[Nahua foodways|Nahua]] families in the Huasteca fill theirs with beans and call them piquiz, and [[Garifuna foodways|Garifuna]] darasa use green banana in place of corn.
