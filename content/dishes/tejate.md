A cold drink of [[Nixtamalization|nixtamalized]] corn, toasted [[Cacao|cacao]], mamey seeds and rosita de cacao flowers, ground to a dough and worked by hand with water until pale curds float on top. It's made mostly by [[Zapotec foodways|Zapotec]] women in Oaxaca's Central Valleys and served in painted gourd bowls, with as much syrup as you like.
