A thick, mildly alcoholic beer of sprouted [[Maize|corn]], called sowiki or batari by the [[Rarámuri foodways|Rarámuri]]. Women sprout and grind the corn, boil it for 10 to 12 hours and ferment it in clay pots over a week or more, and it's shared when neighbors gather to work, heal, plant, harvest or mourn.
