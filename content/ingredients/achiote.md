Red seeds of the achiote tree, soaked and ground into an earthy paste. The [[Maya cuisine|Maya]] used it as a dye and in offerings, and today it colors [[Cochinita pibil]] and [[Kak'ik]]. [[Amazonian Kichwa foodways|Kichwa]] families grow it as manduru, and in Puerto Rico it seasons pasteles alongside [[Taíno heritage foodways|Taíno]] tubers.
