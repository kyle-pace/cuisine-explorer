The nut of the oak, a staple for many [[California Indigenous foodways|California nations]]. Acorns are cracked, ground and leached with water to remove the bitter tannins, then cooked into [[Acorn mush]] or soup, or used as flour. The [[Tohono O'odham foodways|Tohono O'odham]] gather them as a wild food too.
