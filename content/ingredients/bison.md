For the [[Lakota foodways|Lakota]] and other Plains nations, bison gives food, clothing, shelter and tools. The meat is dried, and pounded with fat and berries into [[Pemmican]]. The InterTribal Buffalo Council's 89 member nations are bringing herds back, and many share the meat with elders and families.
