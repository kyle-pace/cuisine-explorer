A flour corn ground into meal across the Southwest. [[Hopi foodways|Hopi]] cooks mix it with ash for [[Piki]], which turns it slate blue instead of lavender, and [[Diné foodways|Diné]] cooks stir juniper ash into [[Blue corn mush]], which adds calcium.
