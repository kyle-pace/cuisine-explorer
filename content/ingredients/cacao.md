The seeds of the cacao tree, drunk in Oaxaca since long before the Spanish came. [[Zapotec foodways|Zapotec]] women grind it toasted with corn for [[Tejate]], [[Mixtec foodways|Mixtec]] cooks make chilate with toasted corn and chile, [[Amazonian Kichwa foodways|Kichwa]] chakras grow it with its wild relatives, and on the islands of Guna Yala many [[Guna foodways|Guna]] families have it every day.
