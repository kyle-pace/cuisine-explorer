A wild bulb of Northwest meadows. [[Coast Salish foodways|Coast Salish]] nations hold intertribal camas harvests that bring Elders and youth together, and [[Nuu-chah-nulth foodways|Nuu-chah-nulth]] families gather the bulbs in spring and summer.
