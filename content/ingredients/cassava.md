A starchy root that came out of South America. [[Taíno heritage foodways|Taíno]] descendants and [[Garifuna foodways|Garifuna]] women grate and dry it for [[Casabe]], [[Amazonian Kichwa foodways|Kichwa]] women ferment it into [[Chicha]], and the [[Guaraní foodways|Guaraní]] relied on it as much as on corn. In Paraguay it is baked into [[Chipá|chipa]].
