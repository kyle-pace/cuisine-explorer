[[Potato|Potatoes]] freeze-dried by the Andean winter. They're left out on frosty nights, trodden to squeeze out the water, and dried in the sun until black and hard, and then they keep for years, even decades. White versions, called tunta or moraya, are soaked in running water. [[Aymara foodways|Aymara]] and [[Quechua foodways|Quechua]] families both make it.
