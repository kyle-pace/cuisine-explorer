Leaves of a native Amazon plant, brewed into a morning drink with about as much caffeine as coffee. [[Amazonian Kichwa foodways|Kichwa]] entrepreneur Esthela Noteno bottles it in Ecuador from her grandmother Juana Andi's recipe.
