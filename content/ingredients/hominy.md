Corn with its hulls loosened by ash or lye. The [[Haudenosaunee foodways|Haudenosaunee]] traditionally hull white corn with hardwood ash, [[Mvskoke foodways|Mvskoke]] cooks make [[Sofkee]] from hominy corn and lye, and hominy is a fixture at [[Cherokee foodways|Cherokee]] wild onion dinners.
