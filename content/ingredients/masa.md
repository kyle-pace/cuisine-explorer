Dough of [[Nixtamalization|nixtamalized]] corn, ground wet. [[Maya cuisine|Maya]] families in Yucatán wrap it around a filling for [[Pib]], [[Zapotec foodways|Zapotec]] women grind corn with cacao for [[Tejate]], and [[Nahua foodways|Nahua]] cooks in Zongolica roll it into little balls for tesmole.
