Cooking dried corn with lime or wood ash, which loosens the hulls and makes the corn more nourishing. It's the first step toward [[Masa]], tortillas and [[Tejate]], and UNESCO counts it as one of the foundations of traditional Mexican cuisine. In [[Nahua foodways|Nahuatl]], the treated corn dough is nextamalli. Farther north, the same idea gives [[Hominy]].
