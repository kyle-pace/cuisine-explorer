A seed crop of the high Andes. [[Quechua foodways|Quechua]] farmers in the Potato Park near Cusco grow it beside their potatoes, families higher up trade potatoes for it, and on Lake Titicaca [[Aymara foodways|Aymara]] fishers trade fish for it on market days.
