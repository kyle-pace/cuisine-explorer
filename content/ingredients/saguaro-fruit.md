Bahidaj, the fruit of the saguaro cactus, is gathered by the [[Tohono O'odham foodways|Tohono O'odham]] in midsummer with long poles made from saguaro ribs. The harvest opens the O'odham new year, and the pulp is cooked into syrup, jam and wine.
