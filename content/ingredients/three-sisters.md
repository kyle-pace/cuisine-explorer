[[Maize|Corn]], [[Bean|beans]] and [[Squash (plant)|squash]], planted together so each helps the others grow. The [[Haudenosaunee foodways|Haudenosaunee]] grow white corn this way, [[Pueblo foodways|Pueblo]] farmers grow all three for meals and ceremony, and [[Cherokee foodways|Cherokee]] cooks simmer them into Three Sisters stew.
