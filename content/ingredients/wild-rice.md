Manoomin, the good berry in Ojibwe, is a native grass that grows in shallow lakes and slow streams around the Great Lakes. [[Anishinaabe foodways|Anishinaabe]] ricers knock the grain into small boats with cedar sticks, and it's food for body and spirit. It also turns up in [[Métis foodways|Métis]] wild rice pancakes.
