How World cuisine works

The recipe for turning Wikipedia’s list of cuisines into a globe.

kylepace.dev/world-cuisine/about

Ingredients

Wikipedia, Wikidata, FAOSTAT, Wikimedia Commons, and notes added by hand

Method

  1. Read the list
  2. Look each one up
  3. Add where things grow
  4. Fold in the handwritten parts
  5. Taste before serving
  6. Plate it
